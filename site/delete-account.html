Tinted

Delete your Tinted account

In the app (instant)

  1. Open Tinted and sign in.
  2. Tap Me, then Delete my account.
  3. Type DELETE and tap Delete my account.

Without the app

Email __SUPPORT_EMAIL__ from the email address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

If you have Plus

Deleting your account doesn't cancel your subscription. Cancel it first in the Google Play Store app: tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions.

What gets deleted

Your profile, photos, reveal facts, matches, messages and duel history are deleted permanently. Reports involving your account may be kept for up to 12 months to protect other users, then deleted. Backups roll off within 30 days.
